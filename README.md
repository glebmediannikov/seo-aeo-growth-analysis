# SEO & AEO Growth Analysis

## Business question

How can a marketing team connect **organic search growth, content discoverability, AI-referred traffic, and conversion performance** in one decision-oriented view?

This project uses a **synthetic dataset** modeled on the types of KPIs I work with professionally. It contains no employer or client data.

## Executive summary

This project demonstrates how I approach SEO and AEO performance as a business problem rather than a traffic-reporting exercise.

The analysis connects search visibility, organic traffic, conversion performance, indexed content, and emerging AI-referred traffic to answer one core question:

**Which areas of search and content investment are most likely to drive measurable business impact?**

The dataset is synthetic, but the analytical framework reflects the types of KPIs and decision-making models I use in professional marketing environments.

## What the project demonstrates

- SEO and AEO performance measurement
- Organic-traffic and conversion analysis
- Search visibility and content-indexation tracking
- Emerging AI-referral monitoring
- Python-based KPI analysis
- Translation of metrics into marketing recommendations

## Example decision framework

The goal is not to report traffic in isolation. The analysis asks:

1. Is organic visibility growing?
2. Is that visibility producing qualified traffic?
3. Is traffic converting?
4. Which content types and intents should receive more investment?
5. Is AI-assisted discovery becoming a meaningful acquisition source?

## Why this matters

Modern search strategy increasingly spans traditional SEO, structured content, AI-assisted discovery, conversion journeys, and measurement. Marketing leaders need one framework connecting these signals to business outcomes.

## Portfolio note

All data in this repository is synthetic and created solely for portfolio demonstration.

## Business recommendations

Based on this framework, a marketing team should:

- Prioritize content that combines strong search demand with high conversion potential
- Separate traffic growth from commercial performance
- Track AI-referred traffic as an emerging acquisition source
- Analyze performance by search intent and landing-page type
- Use ranking, traffic, conversion, and content coverage together rather than as isolated KPIs
